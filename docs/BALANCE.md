# Balance pass — 18 October 2026

The aim is useful, readable spells with different costs, forgiving Gentle play, and real danger in Lively. Quiet aquarium and the default 55-second calm periods remain intact. These are automated measurements and visual reviews, not child playtesting or a guarantee of difficulty for every player.

## What changed and why

- **Song:** the old school slots could extend outside the shrinking circle, and the fish speed cap was below Sabrina's singing speed. Onset is now 0.5 seconds, maximum reach 6.5 units, shrinking with `sqrt(energy / 100)`. Slots stay within reach; fish turn and accelerate into the school, with a 6.8-unit speed cap against Sabrina's 5.6. Energy still lasts 12 / 10 seconds and recharges in 20 / 24; exhaustion still locks the song until full. Distant jellyfish attraction is unchanged, so singing has a risk.
- **Wave:** the old impulse lost most of its effect to immediate drag. The wider front now applies a stronger impulse and a 1.1-second current with gentler drag. Song attraction waits until that current ends, so the two abilities no longer immediately cancel one another. Cooldown remains 3 / 4 seconds; jellyfish remain unaffected. Aim away from the visitor, since a badly aimed wave can still push fish into danger.
- **Crystal:** the long 150 / 180-second cooldown is retained. Its supplied model follows a close orbit (1.2-unit horizontal radius, 0.85 depth radius), gathers to the hands, flies with a braided trail and bursts on the jellyfish's growing outer collision envelope. Each cast repels at most one visitor. Rescued fish receive a visible four-second protection bubble including transit; after transit they still collide with jellyfish instead of passing through them.
- **Score:** remove the 75-point cast penalty, which was effectively free when cast at zero points and punished later emergency use. Survival is worth 80–115 per fish as visits advance; species variety remains +60 each. Crystal rescue +80 and a safe wave push +20 remain once per fish per visit. A song escort now gives +30 after a fish heard the song within five units of a visitor and then reaches six units of clearance while hearing it continuously for at least one second. This also scores only once per fish per visit. Repelling +300, natural departure +150 and sting penalty up to 100 are unchanged. These totals intentionally prioritize surviving fish over repeatedly pressing abilities.

## Controlled ability measurements

Baseline is commit `645b9a3`. Both versions use the same Linux release compiler and inputs.

| Scenario | Before | Now |
|---|---:|---:|
| Stationary song, 12 fish initially 4.5 units away, fish within 3.5 units after six seconds (24 seeds) | 5.00 / 12 | 11.92 / 12 |
| Same gathering setup without singing | 0.38 / 12 | 0.38 / 12 |
| Single wave: extra forward travel after 1.4 seconds over the no-wave control, 60 Hz | 0.96 units | 3.01 units |
| Same wave probe, 30 Hz | 0.94 units | 3.02 units |

The wave probe starts Sabrina at (-7, 8), a 0.6-size right-facing fish at (-3, 8.5), with no other fish or visitors. The regression suite asserts more than two extra units of travel. Gathering results are generated by `balance_report`.

## Five-minute bot sessions

`cmake --build build --target balance_report` then `build/balance_report` runs 24 seeds for each mode/policy, at 60 Hz, up to 300 seconds or game over. `build/balance_report 4` runs only the combined-spells policy plus the gathering control. This report is not a cross-platform golden trace: standard-library random distributions can differ.

The active bot follows a slow oval patrol, steers away from visitors within five units, sings between 90% and 25% energy, casts waves when ready, and uses the crystal after 15 seconds of a visit. It has no route planner or expert wave aim. It intentionally exposes the risk of careless singing and indiscriminate waves.

| Current policy | Gentle: game overs / 24 | Lively: game overs / 24 |
|---|---:|---:|
| Idle | 0 | 1 |
| Patrol only | 0 | 3 |
| Patrol + song | 0 | 9 |
| Patrol + wave | 0 | 3 |
| Patrol + all spells | 1 | 4 |

Combined-spell runs end with mean health 85.8 / 64.9 and banked score 4,085 / 3,216 (Gentle / Lively). Baseline scores were 3,353 / 2,670, but the scoring formula itself changed, so this is **not** evidence of better play. Counts of fish eaten across a whole run also mix different visit counts and early deaths and must not be read as a controlled rescue rate.

The stationary gathering and displacement tests confirm that song and wave are substantially more useful. The patrol shows that Gentle is usually survivable and Lively still punishes luring visitors without a safe exit. It does not establish that automated spell use always beats idle play, nor that the difficulty is final. The next useful feedback is whether a child understands the song boundary, the wave direction and the long crystal recharge during actual play.
