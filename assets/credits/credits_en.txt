Sabrina’s Underwater Fairy Tale
A little sea, from Scratch to 3D

Development and remake
Luka Dežulović

Original idea
Sabrina Dežulović

It started with Sabrina’s idea: a mermaid, tropical fish, a magic crystal, and a little underwater world to look after.

The first version found its home in Scratch. This remake gives that same little sea some more room, with reefs to swim around and fish to keep you company.

Luka is rebuilding it in 3D, keeping the things that made the original feel like Sabrina’s world. There is still a song to sing, a school of fish to protect, and time for a quiet swim when the jellyfish are gone.

It is a small game, still growing. The swimming, the creatures and their little details are works in progress. Thank you for spending a little time here.

Written in Rust. Powered by Bevy. Built with open-source tools.
